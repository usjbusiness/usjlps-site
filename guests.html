03 / Keep it straightforward

A clear process.
A personal conversation.

01 / INTRODUCE

Tell us your idea.

A few sentences about your work and what you could share are enough to begin. You do not need to commit to a date yet.

02 / AGREE

Find the right fit.

We discuss the audience, topic, format, timing and requirements with you before confirming or announcing anything.

03 / PREPARE

Make it worthwhile.

Once agreed, we coordinate the student side: invitations, questions and the session’s flow. You bring your experience, not a sales pitch.

04 / Before you say hello

A few things
worth knowing.

Who can take part?

Professionals, founders, executives, coaches and practitioners with relevant experience and something useful to share. You do not need to be a USJ alum. We discuss the fit for each proposed session.

Is this a recruiting or sales event?

Learning and dialogue come first. Relevant public opportunities can be discussed by agreement, but no hiring commitment or outcome is expected. A guest session is not a sales presentation.

Can I join remotely or bring colleagues?

Both can be discussed. Let us know whether you have a solo conversation, panel or team-led activity in mind, and we will confirm what is feasible together.

What about travel, fees or recording?

Please mention any honorarium, travel, accessibility or technical requirements early. Costs and arrangements must be agreed in advance. Photography, recording and publicity are also discussed with you beforehand; none should be assumed.
