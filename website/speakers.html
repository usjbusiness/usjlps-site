Start a conversation ↗
USJ Leadership & Professional Society · West Hartford, Connecticut
The invitationA conversation. Not a corporate presentation.

Bring the experience.
We'll bring the room.

We invite executives, founders, coaches and public figures to meet motivated USJ students across majors.

Share the decisions, setbacks and turning points that shaped your path. Give students a clearer picture of what comes next — and a real person to ask.

01 / The audience

Talent beyond one major.

Meet students across business, health, science, education, media and tech in one setting.

02 / The conversation

A prepared room.

Members research your work and arrive with questions. Your time goes toward a conversation that matters.

03 / The experience

Hosted with intention.

Promotion, sign-ups and the evening's flow are handled by the Society. Format and logistics are agreed with you beforehand.

04 / The legacy

A path others can follow.

With your permission, share “My path in 5 steps” for the Path Library and future members.

A Typical Evening

Personal. Practical.
Worth the evening.

Usually 45–60 minutes on campus. A remote conversation can be discussed when it is a better fit.

  1. 01

    Your story

    A talk or fireside conversation about your path.

  2. 02

    Their questions

    Student-led Q&A, prepared and moderated.

  3. 03

    A real connection

    A short meet-and-greet when the format allows.

Respect comes first.

Photography, recording and any published quotes are discussed with you in advance. No hiring commitment is expected. Access and attendance depend on the event format.
